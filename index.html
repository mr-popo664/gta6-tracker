Differences between Jason and Lucia’s missions

Grand Theft Auto VI builds its narrative around the dynamic partnership between Lucia and Jason, and each character approaches Leonida’s criminal underworld differently. Lucia’s solo chapters often revolve around social engineering, high-pressure urban escapes in Vice City, and precision heist setups, whereas Jason’s assignments emphasize rural smuggling routes, backcountry surveillance, and heavy off-road transport across Vice County.

Shared Safehouses and Combined Heist Prep

Certain businesses and safehouses are shared between both protagonists once unlocked, pooling passive income and vehicle storage. Our checklist labels every objective with its primary character or shared status so you know immediately which protagonist to switch to before driving to a mission marker.

Why using cheat codes disables trophies

Just like in earlier Rockstar titles, entering any cheat sequence immediately flags your current play session and disables PlayStation trophies and Xbox achievements. Because cheats alter core balance rules—such as removing your wanted level, granting temporary invincibility, or spawning high-tier sports cars like the Infernus Classic—the game prevents players from unlocking completionist awards while modified rules are active.

Always Create a Manual Backup Save First

Before you enter any sequence from the Cheat Code Viewer, pause the game and create a dedicated manual save slot (or quick-save via your in-game phone) with autosave temporarily turned off. Once you finish experimenting, load that clean save file to restore full trophy and achievement eligibility for your 100% run.
